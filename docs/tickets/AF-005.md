## Limitations / next ticket

Redaction is best-effort for owned synthetic data only; unknown formats can escape.
Whole-line redaction can remove useful context. Sanitized file/snapshot hashes are
not raw-source hashes. Bundle revision hashes canonical bundle data excluding its
revision field; the final bundle bytes additionally have a content-addressed object.
No invented commit identifier is used. Hash chains require a trusted external head
to detect truncation/rewrite. Single writer, no concurrent-writer support; bounded
replay currently scans the prefix on each append. Failed publication can leave
unreferenced sanitized objects; partial traces remain invalid and cannot continue.
No auto-cleanup or recovery fabricates a successful final bundle.
